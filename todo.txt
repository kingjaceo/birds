5. Game Design:
	a. A Party has a collection of abilities. On your turn, you can play one ability.
	   That ability is in the discard pile, and won't come back until you skip a turn
	   aftere which you get all abilities back except one.
	b. After your turn, the enemy takes a turn!

6. Bird Types:
	- Gray: chicklings, very basic abilities, choose 3 of 4
	- Blue: bullies, multiple move and push abilities, low damage, choose 4 of 5
	- White: support, nearly no damage, choose 4 of 5
	- Red: ranged, AoE, choose 4 of 5
	- Brown: high melee, move and attack, attack around, choose 4 of 5
	- Black: rule-breakers, stun + additional ability, mass stun, move allies, choose 4 of 5
